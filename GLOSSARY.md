# Customer licensing

These terms describe the fictional business model used by this project.

## Language

**Customer**: An organisation that holds licences. A customer may hold none, one or several.
_Avoid_: Client, licence holder.

**Product**: A named software offering. An inactive product is no longer offered for new sales; existing licences can remain valid.

**Licence**: A grant of seats for one customer and one product over a fixed period. It records an entitlement, not an activation key or a purchase price.
_Avoid_: Subscription, contract, license.

**Seat**: Permission for one user to use one product. A seat allowance does not measure actual users or usage.

**Renewal**: A new licence for the period after an earlier licence ends. The earlier term remains part of the history.

**Cancellation**: An early end to a licence, effective from a specified date. Recording a future cancellation does not end access immediately.

**Active licence**: A licence whose start date has arrived and whose expiry or cancellation has not yet taken effect, evaluated on a specified date.
